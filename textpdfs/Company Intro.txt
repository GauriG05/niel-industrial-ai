COMPANY OVERVIEW:
New India Electricals Limited, established in 1969 and based in Bengaluru, India, describes itself as a leading electrical equipment manufacturer and exporter with over 55 years of service to heavy industry (mining, steel, cement, power & utility) across Africa, Asia and the Middle East.
It exports to more than 45 countries and has been awarded Star Export House status by the Indian Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
Primary focus: the mining industry, steel mills and utility companies, apart from other large industrial sectors.
Stated competitive advantages: cost optimization (experience competing with Chinese manufacturers while maintaining superior quality), immediate availability (over 4,000 motors in stock), established brand partnerships, and one-stop electrical solutions (consolidated procurement; for priority customers, purchasing partner for ancillary accessories and non-electrical items).
Company details on every page: Tel +91 80 42434343, 22235434; Fax +91 80 22226666; Site www.newindiaelectricals.com; CIN U85110KA1994PLC015512; Power House # 18, J.C. Road, Bangalore 560 002, INDIA.

PRODUCTS & SERVICES:
- Switchgear & Control Panels (LV/MV): custom-designed panels for motor and pump control systems, manufactured at the ABB Licensed System House facility with full type-testing capabilities
- Motors & Drive Systems: complete range of AC/DC motors (220V/380V/500V/550V/3300V), including energy-efficient options and specialized heavy-duty motors for harsh environments
- Transformers & Transmission Systems: power and distribution transformers for industrial and utility applications, including step-down transformers for various voltage requirements
- Pumps: industrial pumps for dewatering, slurry handling and process applications for mining, steel, cement and water utility operations
- Substation Equipment: complete substation solutions including CT/PT, power factor correction and protection systems
- Others: Consultation & Design, Power Management Systems, Turnkey Power & Substations Systems up to 230 kV
- Also mentioned: cables (under One-Stop Electrical Solutions: "From switchgear to cables, motors to transformers")
